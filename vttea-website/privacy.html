Privacy Policy

Draft for operator review before launch. Confirm retention, service providers, and deletion practices against the deployed configuration.

Information used

Vttea receives Discord server, channel, user, and voice-state information needed to respond to commands and connect to voice. Server settings can include server, channel, and role identifiers. Playback requests and track links are sent to the configured Lavalink server and may be processed by music providers. Lyrics requests are sent to LRCLIB.

Storage and logs

Server settings are stored in a database. Service providers may retain operational logs. The operator should establish and publish retention periods before launch.

Questions and deletion

Contact shamelepoard@gmail.com to ask about data or request deletion.
